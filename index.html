HOW TO PLAY 操作方法

Weave through traffic. Every near-miss is inked on the road and scores. Chain them for combos, hit ×8 for FEVER. Don't crash.

⌨︎ KEYBOARD

  • ◀ ▶Steer left / right (A / D)
  • ▲Boost (W) — spends the BOOST gauge
  • SHIFTDrift — hold while steering (or SPACE)
  • FFire the cannon — clears a car, spends 1 ammo
  • PPause · M mute · R restart

✈ IN THE AIR (after a ramp)

  • ◀ ▶Barrel roll
  • ▲Front flip
  • SHIFTSpin — land clean for big points

▤ TOUCH / MOBILE

  • ◀ ▶Bottom-left buttons — steer
  • 加速Bottom-right red button — boost
  • DRIFTHold the DRIFT button while steering
  • FIREAbove the steer buttons — one shot per tap

✈ IN THE AIR (mobile)

  • ◀ ▶Roll · 加速 flip · DRIFT spin

↩ CORNERS & SUPPLIES

  • When TURN LEFT / RIGHT flashes, steer that way — hold DRIFT through the apex for triple ink.
  • Yellow 補給 crates hold AMMO or a SHIELD — shoot them or drive through. The shield blocks one crash.
